I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group and am advised by Professor Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning, with a particular interest in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. Professor Junxian He, who previously advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU, is now my Ph.D. supervisor.

Since February 2025 I have been a Research Intern at MINIMAX. I was previously a Research Intern at Tencent WXG (June 2024 &ndash; September 2024), advised by Zifei Shan, and at Shanghai AI Lab (June 2023 &ndash; December 2023), advised by Prof. Yu Cheng.
